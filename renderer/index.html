Demo camera

Camera warm-up

Tap each seed packet and hold the action for a couple of seconds so the camera can see it. In the garden, daylight fills sun needs by itself while you pick one plant and the camera tends it from your smile, voice, breath, and stretch.

  • Smile — waters the bed · hold S
  • Talk — fertilizes · hold T
  • Breathe — slow breath harvests a fully grown crop · runs on its own
  • Neck stretch — stakes tall plants · hold [ / ]

Keyboard mode: tap a packet, then hold the key. F looks away.

Right-click a packet to skip it. When tending, press Esc or go idle to stop.

Before you begin

Smile Garden is a relaxing game for general wellness. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or monitor any health condition. Camera readings are estimates and can be wrong.

The breathing step is always optional. If you have a heart or lung condition, are pregnant, or have felt faint while breathing slowly before, please check with your doctor first, or simply skip it. Breathe gently, never hold your breath, and stop if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable.

Smile Garden is for adults 18 and older.
